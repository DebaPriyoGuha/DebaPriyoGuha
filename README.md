### Deba Priyo Guha

Research Assistant at the Center for Astronomy, Space Science and Astrophysics (CASSA),
Independent University, Bangladesh.

I work on Type Ia supernova cosmology: measuring the stellar ages of supernova host
galaxies from broadband photometry with Bayesian SED fitting, and studying how host
properties relate to supernova distances. I also apply machine learning to astronomical
data, including radio galaxy images, photometric light curves and planetary
hyperspectral data.
